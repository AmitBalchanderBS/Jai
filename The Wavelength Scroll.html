The Invisible Warmth

You are currently in the Infrared spectrum. We cannot see this light, but we feel it as heat. These waves are long and carry less energy.

Wavelength (λ) > 700 nm
Frequency (f) < 430 THz

Entering Visible Light

Red light has the longest wavelength of all visible light. Because it scatters the least in our atmosphere, it penetrates dust and fog, making it ideal for traffic lights and brake lights.

Wavelength (λ) ~ 700 - 635 nm
Frequency (f) ~ 430 - 480 THz

The Solar Peak

Our sun emits light across the entire spectrum, but its energy peaks right here. Human eyes have evolved to be highly sensitive to these exact wavelengths, which is why high-visibility vests use these colors.

Wavelength (λ) ~ 580 - 540 nm
Frequency (f) ~ 517 - 555 THz

High Energy Scattering

Blue light waves are shorter and vibrate faster. When sunlight hits Earth's atmosphere, molecules scatter these highly energetic waves in every direction, painting the sky blue.

Wavelength (λ) ~ 490 - 450 nm
Frequency (f) ~ 610 - 665 THz

Beyond Sight

We are exiting the visible spectrum into Ultraviolet. The frequency is now so high that these waves carry enough energy to damage DNA, which is why we wear sunscreen.

Wavelength (λ) < 400 nm
Frequency (f) > 750 THz
